# Security review 2 (after Phase 7)

**Scope:** everything merged to `main` since security review 1 (PR #15): Phases 5 (levels, missed days, late offline check-ins, XP, badges, leaderboards, group goals, invite details), 6 (the wall, reactions, report and block, nudges, partners, milestone cards, live updates) and 7 (the notification pipeline, push, email, unsubscribe). That is 13 migrations (about 10,000 lines of SQL), 9 new API routes and the new feature modules. Reviewed as a hostile outsider against CLAUDE.md §10 and OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2.

**Method:** read every new migration, route handler and server action; listed every function the `anon` and `authenticated` roles can execute and checked each writing one for a database-side limit (D-051); probed a local Supabase stack as members, admins and outsiders of two groups (SQL as the `authenticated` role, the Realtime socket with only the publishable key); built the app with canary secrets and searched the client bundle; ran gitleaks over the history since the last review. Each finding got a failing test first (commit `test(security): failing tests…`), then the fix.

### SR2-1 · Blocking the moderators (high)

A block (D-078) hides two people from each other in both directions, and `visible_post()` / `visible_comment()` enforce that for everything, including `delete_post()` and `delete_comment()`. So a member can post something cruel, or explicit, then block the owner and every admin. Verified: the report still reaches the owner's moderation page with the copied text, but **Remove** answers `post_not_found` for the owner and for the admin, while every other member keeps seeing the post. Removing the member doesn't help: posts stay up after their author is removed. With the platform report queue not built yet (Phase 12), the only way out was deleting the whole group. The content filter only catches slurs and explicit links, not explicit text, so this defeated the zero-tolerance rule in §7.11.

**Fix:** `private.moderatable_post()` / `private.moderatable_comment()`: an active owner or admin of the post's own group reaches it to remove it, block or not; everyone else goes through `visible_post()` exactly as before, with the same `post_not_found` answer, so nothing opens to members or other groups. Reading is unchanged: the block still hides the post from the admin's wall; they reach it from the report. Removals are audited as before (`post.removed`, `comment.removed`).

### SR2-2 · Push endpoints as an SSRF (medium)

`save_push_subscription()` and the subscribe route accepted any `https://` URL. The sender then POSTs an encrypted payload to it, with the app's VAPID header, every time that person is notified, on up to 10 "devices", from the server's network. Verified: `https://192.168.1.1:8443/admin/reboot` was accepted. That reaches hosts the attacker can't (on the owner's PC today, the home network; in production, anything the host can reach), and the device list gives a one-bit answer back, because a 404 or 410 deletes the subscription at once. A member can trigger their own notifications cheaply (a second account's nudges and reactions), within the 6-an-hour push limit.

**Fix:** only the push services browsers use, on the default port and with no user name: `fcm.googleapis.com` (Chrome, Edge on Android, Samsung, Opera, Brave), `updates.push.services.mozilla.com` (Firefox), `web.push.apple.com` (Safari) and `*.notify.windows.com` (Edge on Windows). Enforced three times: the Zod schema (`src/lib/push/endpoints.ts`), `util.push_endpoint_ok()` in `save_push_subscription()` plus a check constraint on the table, and `sendPush()` itself, which never contacts anything else and reports it "gone" so it's deleted. Existing rows that don't match are deleted by the migration.

### SR2-3 · Comments outlive their post (low)

The page never shows them, but `select * from comments where post_id = …` through the Data API returned comments under a post its author had deleted (a prayer request someone took back, with replies quoting it), under a post whose author had blocked the reader, and under a milestone card no longer shown (D-085).

**Fix:** the comments policy now also requires the post to be readable (`exists (select 1 from posts …)`, so the posts policy applies inside it). `post_comments()` already worked this way.

### SR2-4 · Fake live-update pings (low)

Pings went out on public Realtime channels (D-086). The channel names are secret HMACs, but on a public channel anyone holding the publishable key can also **send**. Verified with two sockets and only the publishable key: 20 pings sent by the "attacker" all arrived at the "member". A member removed today keeps the name until midnight UTC and could keep every open group page refetching (each ping is a server round trip per open page); the same goes for anyone a name leaked to. Pings carry no content, so nothing leaked; this is abuse, not disclosure.

**Fix:** the database now sends private messages (`realtime.send(…, true)`), the two listeners join with `private: true`, and a policy on `realtime.messages` lets browsers (the `anon` role, as they hold only the publishable key, or a signed-in token) **select** only on our own channel names. With no insert policy, a client's broadcast is dropped. Verified again over the socket: the attacker's sends are dropped and the database's ping still arrives.

### SR2-5 · Muting a queued nudge (low)

`run_notify_job` checked `util.takes_nudges(group, user)` with the arguments the wrong way round, so the check always passed. `send_nudge()` itself checks correctly, so this only matters for a nudge still in the queue (a few seconds) when its recipient mutes nudges, which is exactly when someone who feels harassed mutes them.

**Fix:** the arguments are in the right order.

## What held up (attacked, no finding)

- **Another group's data:** posts, comments, reactions, reports, nudges, partnerships, group goals, leaderboard entries and notifications read directly with another group's ids; `group_wall`, `wall_post`, `post_comments`, `group_reports`, `group_leaderboard`, `group_together`, `group_invite_people`, `group_nudge_targets`, `my_partnerships`, `group_checkin_reactions` and `group_live_topic` called with another group's ids or another group's post, comment, check-in, invite and report ids: all empty or `*_not_found`. pgTAP `022` covers each new group function; e2e `group-isolation.spec.ts` passed again.
- **Share levels through the new features:** the level and badges show only at streak or full (`group_checkins_today`, `profile_cards`, the Level board); checkin_only members appear only on the XP and Encourager boards; XP pays a slip exactly like a free day (the check-in streak bonus counts check-ins, not free days); "Together" and the goal count only streak-or-more members; milestone cards and their notifications follow the member's current share level; the check-in id in the Today panel says nothing beyond "checked in".
- **Another person's check-in notes or journal:** notes stay owner-only and AES-GCM encrypted; no view, function, export, notification row, push payload or email carries them; partners see mood, urges and triggers in their group only (`util.are_partners_in`), never notes. Journals don't exist yet (Phase 8).
- **Member to admin:** `pin_post`, `group_reports`, `resolve_report`, `set_group_goal`, `end_group_goal`, `group_invite_people`, removing someone else's post or comment, and announcements, all as a member: 42501. Pending, removed and closing members can't write. `update_my_group_membership` can't go below the group's minimum share level or hide when hiding isn't allowed. The notifier's secret-key functions (`notify_claim`, `notify_finish`, `notify_unsubscribe`, `notify_configure`) refuse members.
- **Invites:** unchanged since review 1 (160-bit tokens and 50-bit HMAC'd codes, hashed). The new invite details are admins-only and keyed to the invite's own group.
- **Script injection:** posts, comments, report notes and display names are stored as typed and rendered by React as text (e2e checks a `<script>`/`onerror` post); links in posts are never made clickable; notification links are built only from UUIDs (`links.ts`); the service worker opens only same-origin paths; email templates are React Email (escaped); the share card draws text with `ImageResponse`. No new `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. Uploads didn't change.
- **Rate limits:** every writing function either role can call is throttled inside the database (`private.throttle`), the only exceptions being Phase 2–3 functions that throttle through `lock_group()`. Nudges also have a once-a-day unique index; partner SOS is 5 an hour; notifications have per-person hourly push and email limits.
- **Secrets:** gitleaks over the 13 commits since review 1: no leaks. A production build with canary values for `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`, `APP_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, `GOOGLE_CLOUD_VISION_API_KEY`, `SMTP_PASSWORD`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`, `CRON_SECRET` and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`: 0 hits in `.next/static` and `public/`. The sender's address and `CRON_SECRET` live in Vault; the route compares the bearer in constant time; unsubscribe links are HMAC-signed and need a POST.

## Accepted or for the owner to decide

- **Late offline check-ins trust the device's clock** within `offline_sync_max_days` (7, D-067): someone can answer an unanswered day from the last week by sending a chosen `recordedAt`. Accepted when it was designed; it's recorded in the threat model and audited as `checkin.synced_late`.
- **A hidden member's place** can sometimes be worked out by elimination in a small group (D-074); owners and admins see the name by design (D-088).
- **Encouragement XP between two friends** (reacting to each other's posts) is capped at +10 a day each (D-071).
- **A removed member keeps today's channel names until midnight UTC.** After SR2-4 they can only hear content-free pings ("the wall changed"), and from tomorrow nothing.
- **An endpoint moves to whoever saves it last** (D-092). Taking over someone's push would need their endpoint, which is never readable through the API (column grants).

## Verification

- pgTAP: 1,015 checks (34 new: `083` 16, `093` 18), all passing after `supabase db reset`; `082`, `090` and `092` updated for private pings and real push-service addresses; `supabase db lint` clean.
- Vitest: 399 tests (21 new), all passing; typecheck, ESLint and Prettier clean.
- Playwright, against a production build, desktop and mobile: `wall`, `live`, `notifications`, `delivery`, `accountability` and `group-isolation` (39 tests) plus the new "an owner the author blocked still removes the reported post": all passing. One mobile run of the bell test first saw 2 unread instead of 1 (the 15-minute scheduler's "challenge starts today" notice landing mid-test, a clock flake unrelated to this change); it passed on re-run.
- The SR2-1, SR2-2, SR2-3 and SR2-4 attacks were re-run by hand after the fix and are now refused.
